AI Tooling Disclosure, Verification Guardrails, Accounting Notes, Data Gaps, and All 70 Archived Sources

1. How This Site Was Built (AI + Reproducible Pipeline Disclosure)

This website and companion PDF brief were initiated, steered, and reviewed by Matthew R.V. Ross (CSU faculty) using Google DeepMind’s Antigravity coding agent to build an open-source, reproducible R (4.5.2) + Quarto (1.10) pipeline (src/00_setup_theme.R through src/05_verify_audit.R). They are shared humbly ahead of late-2026 governance meetings in the hope that having verified numbers in one transparent repository helps colleagues across faculty, staff, and administration discuss the same baseline—and quickly spot anything we should refine or correct.

Because both automated tools and fast-moving campus commentary can easily introduce errors or blind spots, six explicit reality checks were built into the workflow:

  1. Primary-Source-Only Ingestion (70 SHA-256 Checksummed Files): Script 01_fetch_sources.R archived 70 public PDFs, governance memos, and federal/BLS API datasets from CSU Office of Budgets, CSU IRP&E Fact Books, CDHE Format 30/20 Budget Data Books, CSU System Audited Financial Statements (FY15–FY25), Colorado Office of the State Auditor (OSA) statutory NCAA Agreed-Upon Procedures reports (2516F-B, 2505F-B), BLS CPI-U (CUUR0000SA0), and Urban Institute IPEDS (2015–2023), logging exact URLs, byte sizes, and SHA-256 digests in data/sources_manifest.csv.
  2. Visual Slide Inspection (Zero Estimation on Blank Online Exports): Automated PDF text extraction missed embedded images in 2026 campus budget forum decks, so slide pages were rasterized and inspected visually. That check revealed that Slides 14–15 of the posted Oct. 14, 2026 PDF contained blank white scenario table areas online—so Ledger A draws exclusively from the Oct. 7, 2026 campus memo and May 13 V4 Slide 6 ($15.112M expiring one-time stopgaps) rather than estimating missing values.
  3. Separating Sponsored Research, Clinical & Auxiliary Staff: Early exploratory summaries risked grouping CSU’s all-funds staff ratio (~3:1) under general administrative overhead. Domain review explicitly separated externally and self-funded roles (Table 123 and Table 124), documenting that 69.7% (3,205.5 of 4,597.7) of FY25 Administrative Professional FTE are funded outside E&G across research centers, clinical services, extension, forestry, housing, and auxiliaries (such as CIRA, CEMML, VTH, AES, Extension, and CSFS).
  4. Controls for Cross-Year Accounting Shifts: CSU Office of Budgets ColOBS tables include two structural accounting shifts: moving $41.64M of financial aid from XA Central into P1 Enrollment & Access in FY19, and decentralizing ~$111.26M of pooled XA Employee Fringe Benefits into individual divisions in FY26. Both shifts are documented and controlled in Figure 5 so accounting transfers are not mistaken for sudden spending increases.
  5. Symmetrical Fact-Checking, Humility & Non-Interpolation: Circulating claims from multiple perspectives were checked against public filings (confirming Parsons’ $1.1M settlement terms, Coach Mora’s $2.4M base contract, Athletics’ $75.24M gross vs. ~$15.57M net operating support, and $206.96M Athletics debt; clarifying that San Francisco Chronicle Title IX reporting on Jeffrey Lamoureaux involved universities outside CSU; leaving omitted FY21–FY24 E&G State Classified rows in Fact Book Table 128 as NA without interpolation; and noting openly that aggregate FTE tables cannot judge the operational value of student support, compliance, or continuing faculty career stabilization).
  6. Automated Reconciliation Suite (src/05_verify_audit.R) & Layout Audits: Seven programmatic assertions check ledger balances ($54.272M V4 closeout sums to $0; $61.3M–$70.7M FY28 steps reconcile; FY27 unit cuts equal 160.58 FTE; Table 124 AP FTE reconciles to 1,392.2 E&G / 3,205.5 non-E&G; OSA revenues/expenses/debt balance), alongside HTML DOM checks and visual inspection of figures and PDF tables.

If any colleague across faculty, staff, administration, or the Board sees a nuance, accounting detail, or primary source that would improve this reference, feedback or pull requests at github.com/matthewross07/csu_budget_context are warmly welcomed.

2. Accounting Scope & Deflation Notes

  1. CSU System vs. Fort Collins Standalone Scope (finstmt2015–2025 vs. BDB/ColOBS/OSA):
    • CSU System Audited Statements combine Fort Collins, CSU Pueblo, and System/SPUR ($644.9M net tuition, 21.3% discount rate, $1,330.7M long-term debt in FY25 + $79.1M STRATA ATLAS Laser bonds issued June 26, 2025).
    • Fort Collins Standalone Scope comes from CDHE Format 30/20 Budget Data Books (FINAL_BDB), CSU Office of Budgets Operating Budget Summaries (ColOBS + OBS), IRP&E Fact Books (Tables 122–128), and Note 5 of Colorado OSA Report 2516F-B ($1,118.77M Fort Collins total debt, including $206.96M Athletics debt).
  2. Real-Dollar Deflation (CPI-U, macro_cpiu_fy.csv):
    • Monthly BLS CPI-U All Urban Consumers (CUUR0000SA0, 2014–Oct 2026) is averaged across CSU’s July–June fiscal calendar so multi-year net tuition and debt series are evaluated in constant FY25 or FY26 purchasing power (FY27 covers Jul–Oct 2026).

5. Reproducibility Commands

To re-run the entire pipeline from scratch inside CSU_finance/:

# 1. Fetch/verify all 70 primary sources, parse PDFs/APIs, generate figures, and run 7 audit assertions
Rscript src/run_all.R

# 2. Render standalone PDF brief into outputs/pdf/
quarto render report.qmd --to pdf
mv CSU_Budget_Context_FY27_28.pdf outputs/pdf/CSU_Budget_Context_FY27_28.pdf

# 3. Render static Quarto website into docs/ (configured with robots.txt + noindex headers)
quarto render
